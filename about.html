About Near You

Near You is a mock museum discovery website created as a portfolio project. It helps visitors browse museums, search by location, and save favorites while showing practical front-end design and interaction skills.

Purpose

This site was designed to feel professional, welcoming, and easy to use. The goal is to make museum information feel organized and approachable for visitors on desktop, tablet, and mobile screens.

Features

Visitors can search for nearby museums, view detailed museum information, favorite museums, and revisit their saved favorites from the homepage.
